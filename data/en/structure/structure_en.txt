General Parameters
1.1 Wingspan and Chord
Reference: https://www.uavcoach.com/drone-parts/ (UAV Coach tutorial on drone wing structures, including wingspan, chord, and design parameters)

Wingspan (b):

Small UAV: 1.2–2.5 m

Medium UAV: 2.5–5.5 m

Large UAV: ≥5.5 m

Mean Chord (c):

Ratio Range: Wingspan ÷ 6–12

Example: 5 m wingspan → chord approx. 0.42–0.83 m

Main Spar Design Values:

Spar Height (h_spar): 0.05–0.15 × chord (m)

Spar Width (t_spar): 0.01–0.03 × chord (m)

Material Density: Carbon fiber 1.5 g/cm³, Aluminum 7075 alloy 2.8 g/cm³

Flexural Modulus (E): Carbon fiber 70–250 GPa, Aluminum alloy 69–72 GPa

Allowable Stress (σ_allow): Carbon fiber 600–1500 MPa, Aluminum alloy 300–500 MPa

Design Safety Factor (SF): 1.5–2.5

1.3 Ribs
Reference: https://ntrs.nasa.gov (NASA Technical Report Server – for UAV structure and aerodynamic load data)

Frequency: 1 rib every 0.15–0.3 m

Thickness: 6–10 mm

Material: Carbon/Kevlar patch + lightweight foam filling

Strength Goal: Sustain 150% aerodynamic distributed load without local buckling

1.4 Skin
Reference: https://www.makeitfrom.com (Material property data for skin materials like carbon fiber)

Material: Carbon fiber prepreg composite, 3–8 layers

Thickness: 0.3–1.5 mm (thinner at wingtip, thicker at root)

Safety Factor: 1.8–2.5 (higher in reinforced zones)

Fatigue Life: Over 50,000 takeoff-landing cycles

1.5 Ailerons
Reference: https://www.aerodynamics4students.com/control-surfaces/ailerons.php

Length: 20–30% of wingspan

Chord Length: 18–25% of mean chord

Hinge Position from Wing Root: 80% of semi-span

Torsional Stiffness: GJ ≥ 5000 N·m² (sufficient for high-speed operation loads)

2. Airframe
2.1 Geometric Dimensions
Reference: https://www.militaryfactory.com

Max Size: Length ≤ 1.5 m, Width ≤ 0.6 m, Height ≤ 0.3 m

Wall Thickness (Skin + Internal Supports): 2–5 mm

2.2 Material Properties
Reference: https://www.matweb.com

Density: CFRP 1.5–1.8 g/cm³; Aluminum Alloy 2.7–2.8 g/cm³

Flexural Modulus: Similar to wing

Allowable Stress:

CFRP: 400–1200 MPa (depending on ply orientation)

Aluminum Alloy: 250–450 MPa

2.3 Structural Configuration

Semi-monocoque structure + stiffeners/braces (spaced every 100–150 mm)

Curvature/Stiffening Ribs: Maintain torsional rigidity

2.4 Fatigue and Lifetime

Design Life: 50,000–100,000 flight cycles

Fatigue Safety Margin: Goodman limit = 0.6

3. Landing Gear
3.1 Type
Reference: https://www.dronedesignerhub.com/drone-landing-gear-design/

Fixed type; U-shaped arms made from carbon fiber or aluminum alloy

3.2 Bending and Impact

Material Stiffness: E = 30–90 GPa

Design Stress Capacity: σ_allow ≥ 400 MPa

Drop Energy Absorption: mgh ≈ 0.5·m·v², v ≈ 1.2 m/s

Shock Absorption Travel: ≥ 30–50 mm

4. Payload Bay
4.1 Dimensional Standards
Reference: https://www.uavsystemsinternational.com

Internal Volume: ≥ 250 × 200 × 100 mm

Opening Size: ≥ 200 × 150 mm

Door Design: Hinged + quick-lock, must resist wind loads

4.2 Load Capacity

Uniform Load: ≥ 5.5 kg

Point Load: ≥ 10 kg

Vibration Standard: MIL-STD-810F

4.3 Structural Interface

Quick-release rail width: 20–30 mm

Interface Tolerance: ±0.2 mm (within 1 m length)

Reinforcement Plate Thickness: 3–6 mm (CFRP or Aluminum)

5. Center of Gravity (CG) Tolerance
Empty CG Offset Tolerance: ±30 mm (longitudinal axis)

Post-Payload Offset Tolerance: ±20–30 mm, should remain near original CG

Installation Tip: Use rails to adjust payload CG

6. Manufacturing Tolerances
Skin Seam Gap: ≤ 0.5 mm

Fastener Hole Tolerance: H7/H8

Corner Radius: R ≥ 3–5 mm (to avoid stress concentration)

7. Fatigue Life Indicators
CFRP Structural Fatigue Life: Tens of thousands to 100,000 cycles

Aluminum Alloy Fatigue Limit: ~30–50% of σ_allow

8. Summary
Dimensional Parameters:

Wingspan: 1.2–5.5 m; Chord Ratio: 6–12

Fuselage Size: Length ≤ 1.5 m, Width ≤ 0.6 m, Height ≤ 0.3 m

Material Densities:

CFRP: 1.5–1.8 g/cm³; Aluminum Alloy: 2.7–2.8 g/cm³

Mechanical Properties:

Spar E ≥ 130 GPa; σ_allow ≥ 600 MPa

Airframe E ≥ 60 GPa; σ_allow ≥ 300 MPa

Landing Gear σ_allow ≥ 400 MPa; Payload Interface σ_allow ≥ 500 MPa

Structural Tolerances and Precision:

CG Offset Tolerance ±30 mm; Rail ±0.2 mm; Skin Gap ≤ 0.5 mm

Design Life:

≥ 50,000 flight cycles; Fatigue Safety Factor ≥ 1.5
