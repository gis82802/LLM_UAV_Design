Key Points for 1.3 Motor Selection
KV value definition: The number of RPM the motor speed increases when the voltage rises by 1V

Torque constant: Kt = 1/KV (unit: N⋅m/A)

2. Propeller Specifications
2.1 Propeller Size Classification


Classification by Diameter
3-inch Propeller: Diameter 76mm, suitable for frames below 150mm
4-inch Propeller: Diameter 102mm, suitable for 180mm frame
5-inch Propeller: Diameter 127mm, suitable for 210mm frame
6-inch Propeller: Diameter 152mm, suitable for 250mm frame
7-inch Propeller: Diameter 178mm, suitable for 350mm frame


Categorized by number of blades
Two-blade propeller: Highest efficiency, fast speed, minimal vibration
Three-blade propeller: Good balance, moderate thrust, most widely used
Four-blade propeller: High thrust, quick response, suitable for aerobatic flights


2.2 Propeller Material Characteristics
Plastic Propeller
Material: ABS, PC, Nylon, etc.
Advantages: Low cost, lightweight, good toughness, not easy to hurt people
Disadvantages: Relatively low strength, prone to deformation at high temperatures
Applications: Beginners, indoor flying, small aircraft
Carbon Fiber Propeller
Material: Carbon Fiber Reinforced Polymer (CFRP)
Advantages: High strength, lightweight, good rigidity, high-temperature resistance
Disadvantages: High cost, fragile, safety needs attention
Applications: Racing machines, high-power applications, professional use

3.3 Battery Selection Calculation
Flight Time Estimation
Flight time (minutes) = Battery capacity (mAh) / Average current (mA) × 60 × 0.8
Where 0.8 is the safety factor
Current requirement calculation
Total current requirement = Number of motors × Current per motor × 1.2
1.2 is the safety margin

5. System matching and calculation
5.1 Thrust-to-weight ratio calculation
Basic formula
Thrust-to-weight ratio = Total thrust / Total weight
Recommended ratio
Drone: 2:1~3:1
Racing aircraft: 4:1~6:1
Special machine: 6:1~10:1


5.2 Power demand calculation
Basic formula
Total power demand = Number of motors × Power per motor × Load coefficient
Load coefficient reference
Hovering: 0.3~0.5
Normal flight: 0.5~0.7
Aggressive flight: 0.7~1.0
